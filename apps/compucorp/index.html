Compucorp 665 Web Emulator & Omega Reference

3.79 MHz Z80 · 64K + 4×64K Paged RAM · 80×20 CRT · Dual-Door 315 KB Floppies · WebAssembly Core

Hardware-level emulator for the Compucorp 665 word processor running ZEBRA 7.04 and the Omega word processing system, compiled from the C reference core to WebAssembly. Disk writes stay in browser memory; use Save As on any drive card to download modified .img images.

Omega Quick Start: Drive A: holds boot.img (Omega Boot Disk) and Drive B: holds docs.img (Document Disk). Click Power. At real speed, booting takes ~119 seconds (screen stays blank for the final minute while loading overlays); toggle Fast (Alt+F or ScrollLock) to boot in ~1 second! At the prompts, enter Time (10/30 AM or Enter) and Date (11/24/26 or Enter) to open a blank Omega page.
